Collaged Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature ideas? Email branpurn@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Quick answers

How do I make a collage? Tap Pick Photos and choose 1 to 9 photos. They are placed in an even grid right away.

How do I change the layout? Drag any line, or the dot where lines meet. Tap a line to show Rotate and flip its split. Pick a shape (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, 4:3, 2:3, Letter) along the bottom.

How do I adjust a photo? Drag it to move it within its frame and pinch to zoom. Tap it for Swap, Rotate, Lock and Delete.

Where does my collage go? Save puts a high-resolution image in Photos; Share sends it anywhere. If saving fails, open Settings > Collaged and allow adding to Photos.
