Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Collaged does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the app does not connect to the internet or send your data to us or to any third party.

Photos

You choose photos with the system photo picker, so Collaged only sees the photos you pick. If you save a collage, it asks for permission to add it to your photo library. Everything stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Data storage

Collaged does not keep copies of your photos or collages. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Collaged does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
