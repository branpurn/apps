Holdcut Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature ideas? Email branpurn@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Quick answers

How do I record? Press and hold the shutter. Let go to pause. Each hold becomes a clip (up to 15 seconds each, 60 seconds per project).

How do I change a clip? Tap a thumbnail in the strip to add a caption or change its audio. You can reorder, insert, retake, or delete clips.

Where do my videos go? Projects stay on your iPhone. Use Export to save the finished video to Photos or Files, or share it.

Camera or microphone not working? Open Settings > Holdcut and allow Camera and Microphone access.
