Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Holdcut does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the app does not send your data to us or to any third party.

Camera, microphone and Photos

Holdcut asks for camera and microphone access only to record your clips. If you choose to save a video, it asks for permission to add it to your photo library. Videos you import are picked by you with the system photo picker. Everything you record or import stays on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

Data storage

Your projects are stored locally on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Holdcut does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
